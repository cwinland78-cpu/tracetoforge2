## Outline

### Quick Answer (60-80 words, top of post, callout-styled)
> **Quick answer:** Custom Gridfinity bins from a photo: place your tool on a sheet of paper, take a top-down phone photo, upload to a photo-based Gridfinity generator (TracetoForge, Tooltrace, or GridPilot), and the app traces the outline using OpenCV edge detection. Set your dimensions, pick Gridfinity Bin mode, and export STL. Print in PETG at 0.2mm layer height. Total time: under 5 minutes from photo to sliced file.
