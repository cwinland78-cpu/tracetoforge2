## Outline

### Quick Answer (callout, top)
> **Quick answer:** Four photo-based Gridfinity generators are worth considering in 2026: **TracetoForge** (multi-tool support, in-browser, freemium), **Tooltrace.ai** (focused on tool/foam shadow boxes), **GridPilot** (AI-powered detection, paid), and **gridfinity.tools** (combines parametric and photo). All four take a phone photo of a tool on paper and output a Gridfinity bin with a precision cutout. They differ in pricing, multi-tool support, export formats, and workflow speed. Pick TracetoForge for free multi-tool layouts and STL/3MF/SVG/DXF; Tooltrace for foam-shadowbox dual mode; GridPilot for stacking/labels; gridfinity.tools if you also need parametric bins.

### When to Pick TracetoForge
- You want a free path from photo to STL
- You're tracing multiple tools into a single tray
- You need SVG (laser) or DXF (CNC) output, not just STL
- You don't want any photo to leave your browser

### When to Pick Tooltrace.ai
- Foam shadow boxes are your primary use case (not just Gridfinity)
- You're already in the tool-foam ecosystem

### When to Pick GridPilot
- You need built-in label and stacking-foot generation
- AI-detection accuracy is your top priority

### When to Pick gridfinity.tools
- You want both parametric and photo-based in one tool
- You're designing simple bins more often than tool-shaped cutouts
